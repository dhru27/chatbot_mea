# Electives Policy — IIT Bombay Mechanical Engineering

Last verified: 2026-06-23. Source: Department General Secretary (Keshav) and official ME documents.

## Graduation Credit Requirements (BTech Vanilla, without honors)

- 2022 Batch: 276 Credits
- 2023 Batch: 264 Credits
- 2024 Batch Onwards: 266 Credits

Note: 6 credits of HS250/HS200, ES250/ES200 count as core. First-year HASMED courses count as core. Data as per official documents on the ME website.

## Elective Breakdown

Electives worth 84 credits need to be completed in addition to core requirements:
- 5 Department Electives (DE)
- 5 Open / Flexible Electives
- 2 HASMED Electives
- 2 STEM Electives

## Category-wise Rules

**Common rule:** Advanced courses from other departments cannot be taken as electives.

### 1. Department Electives
- Must be ME tagged (MExxx)
- Must be ME3xx and above; cannot be a core course
- Can be done via NPTEL/SWAYAM if equivalence exists and is approved by the associated faculty. Max 2 for regular students, 4 for ARP/extension students.
- One SLP/RnD project done in ME can be tagged as DE (2022 batch only).
- One ME-related course from another department can be tagged as DE if present in the DUGC approved list (2022 batch only).

### 2. Open Electives
- Any tag allowed including ME
- One course can be 1xx or 2xx; all others must be 3xx and above
- Project-based courses from any department are NOT allowed
- One 6-credit elective can be replaced by two 3-credit electives
- One SLP/RnD from another department can be tagged as Open Elective (2022 batch only)
- Can be done via NPTEL/SWAYAM with faculty approval. Max 2 for regular, 4 for ARP.
- Some DE courses can be tagged as open (DE335, DE338, DE340, DE344) but NOT HASMED.

### 3. HASMED Electives
- Identified as "Interdisciplinary HASMED course" on internal ASC
- Must be from HS, SOM, DE, ENT, DH departments
- One 6-credit course can be replaced by two 3-credit courses

### 4. STEM Electives
- Identified as "Interdisciplinary STEM course" on internal ASC
- Must be 3xx and above

## General Retagging Guidelines

1. Any "M" tagged Minor course can be retagged to Elective without permission, except DS203, DS303, and MG courses (restricted by respective departments).
2. Electives cannot be retagged to minor directly. Requires prior permission from course instructor and approval from Dean AP via SAF.
3. Courses of 4 and 8 credits can be taken, but permission to exceed the final credit limit needs to be taken from HOD and Dean AP office via SAF.
4. Branch changers can tag one of their previous department DICs as Open Elective (not both).
5. BTP-1 and BTP-2 credits count ONLY towards Honors requirements (ALC otherwise). They cannot be tagged as Electives.

## Honors Degree

To get an honors degree, 24 credits of master-level study are needed. Options:
1. BTP-1 + BTP-2 + one Master-level course (6 + 12 + 6 = 24)
2. BTP-1 + 3 Master-level courses (6 + 18 = 24)
3. 4 Master-level courses (4 × 6 = 24)

Master-level courses are 6xx and above. If BTP is done in a department other than ME, the honors degree is still given in ME. The degree states "BTech in Mechanical Engineering with Honors".

## NPTEL Process

1. Find a 12-week NPTEL course on https://onlinecourses.nptel.ac.in/ run by any IIT faculty (hard rule).
2. Fill the Student NPTEL Course Equivalence form. Mention all planned courses for the semester with total credits.
3. Mention the Course Name and Course Code clearly.
4. Provide the equivalent 6-credit IITB course(s). These can be from any department or IIT.
5. Get the signature of the IITB faculty who has taught the equivalent course.
6. Get the signature of your Faculty Advisor (for checking credit limits and required tag).
7. Signatures of HoD and Dean AP are not necessary at this stage.
8. Register on the NPTEL website via LDAP only. Use the same name as on your IITB ID Card.

Post completion: DGSec will float a form to upload your passing certificate. The equivalent IITB course will be added to your transcript with %marks grade and the respective tag.
